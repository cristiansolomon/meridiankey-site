About MeridianKey

MeridianKey is an informational Android application designed to help users explore traditional meridian concepts and the energy points associated with each meridian.

The app can be used as a complementary reference tool for study, personal reflection, and muscle-testing practices.

What you can explore

Meridians

Navigate through the main and extraordinary meridians.

Energy points

Explore the points associated with a selected meridian.

Point information

Review point names, virtues, disharmonies, and connections.

Open testing

MeridianKey is currently available in open testing on Google Play. Feedback from practitioners and other users can help improve future versions of the application.
