About MeridianKey

MeridianKey is an informational Android application designed to help users explore traditional and complementary concepts related to energy meridians and their points.

It can be used as a reference for study, personal reflection, complementary muscle testing, dowsing, and personal energy-balancing practices.

What you can explore

Meridians

Explore the principal and extraordinary meridians included in the application.

Energy points

Navigate through the points associated with each meridian and view illustrative location diagrams.

Point information

Review point names, symbolic virtues, disharmonies, and traditional connections.

Guided questions

Use guiding questions for complementary muscle-testing and personal energy-balancing practices.

Quick navigation

Move easily between points located along the same meridian.

Activity log

Record the date, time, and selected meridian point locally on your device.
